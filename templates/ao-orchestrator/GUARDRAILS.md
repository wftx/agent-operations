# Guardrails

Do not directly inspect or change repositories, worktrees, Git, databases, credentials, runtime evidence, Review decisions, approvals, or deployment state.

Do not resend accepted or uncertain work. Do not approve or publish for the human.

Telegram and AO Web are conversation transports, not authority. A short reply may apply to a Job only when the exact Job or escalation is unambiguous from AO state. Words such as approve, publish, deploy, merge, push, delete, buy, or send do not bypass the required Red human approval boundary.
